# AI4S competition audit — 2026-09-28

## Live status

- Competition is open at the time of checking.
- Exact Kaggle deadline: 2026-10-10 15:59:59 UTC (2026-10-10 23:59:59 Beijing; 2026-10-10 08:59:59 PDT).
- Preliminary: Aug 15–Oct 10; expert review: Oct 10–20; online final: Oct 20–30; results before Nov 2026.
- Extra registration form is active and is mandatory before Writeup submission.

## Required package

- Kaggle Writeup with category declaration.
- Public demo video, no login/paywall, maximum five minutes.
- Public reproducible repository with environment, model acquisition, test/inference/evaluation/demo entry points and I/O documentation.
- Self-contained technical report, ideally 15–20 pages excluding references and appendices.
- Optional public interactive demo.

## Rubric

- Problem Importance & Potential Impact: 30%.
- Technical Approach & Innovation: 30%.
- Results & Validation: 20%.
- Reproducibility & Implementation Quality: 10%.
- Presentation Quality: 10%.

## Rule inconsistencies and caution

- Text rules say 1–5 members; a Kaggle technical setting was observed to allow a larger maximum. Follow the stricter written 1–5 rule.
- A cross-disciplinary `+0.5` statement references an interpretability/reliability dimension that is not separately present in the published five-part rubric. Do not fabricate a teammate or count on this bonus.
- The organizer rules state that submitted work is jointly owned by the entrant/team and organizing committee and may be displayed publicly by the committee. User acceptance is required before joining.
- The current Writeups page lists submitted projects only as anonymized Team numbers and marks them `Viewable at Hackathon close`; their content cannot yet be inspected. No claim about current leading methods or strong submissions is therefore evidence-supported before the close.
